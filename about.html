Founder & Chief Blockchain Digital Asset Officer

Adilah Holivay

Adilah Holivay is an award-winning blockchain executive, financial systems strategist, and regulatory advisory leader serving as the Founder and Chief Blockchain Digital Asset Officer of DNA Legacy Blockchain Brokerage LLC (DLBB) - a premier blockchain advisory and digital asset solutions firm specializing in blockchain forensics, compliance, risk management, wallet governance, and cryptocurrency investigations.

With more than 12 years of experience across financial services, fintech, and enterprise transformation, Adilah has worked on complex initiatives impacting banking systems, fiduciary spending, compliance, operational modernization, and technology implementation - including engagements with Deloitte and major financial institutions.

She also serves as CEO and board member for the Association for Women in Cryptocurrency (AWIC), championing inclusive leadership, education, and innovation in blockchain technology, and is a sought-after speaker and moderator bridging traditional finance with emerging technology.
